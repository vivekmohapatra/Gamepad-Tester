Light in front, not behind

A small webcam sensor raises its gain in a dim room, and raised gain means visible noise and flat, washed-out colour. A lamp or a window facing you fixes more than a more expensive camera would. A bright window behind you turns you into a silhouette.

Why 1080p often arrives as 720p

The browser negotiates a stream rather than demanding a maximum, and lighting, USB bandwidth and the camera's own frame rate rules all push it down. A 1080p webcam on a shared USB 2.0 hub routinely settles at 720p. Plugging straight into the machine usually restores it.

Camera height matters more than megapixels

Raise the camera to eye level and put your eyes roughly a third of the way down the frame. A stack of books under a laptop does more for how you come across on a call than any hardware upgrade on the market.

How to use the webcam test

Two minutes here saves the first two minutes of a meeting. Check the picture, the resolution and the framing before anyone is watching.

  1. Allow the camera

    Press Start camera and accept the browser prompt. Your camera's own indicator light should come on, which is a useful check in itself.

    If nothing happens, another application is probably holding the camera. Close Zoom, Teams, Discord and OBS and try again.

  2. Check the picture and the numbers

    The panel reports the resolution and frame rate you are actually getting, which is often lower than the camera's advertised maximum.

    Frame rate drops in low light because the camera lengthens its exposure to compensate. If the number looks low, add light before you blame the camera.

  3. Frame yourself, then snapshot

    Put your eyes roughly a third of the way down the frame and raise the camera to eye level. Both do more for how you look on a call than any camera upgrade.

    Take a snapshot to judge it properly. The mirrored preview is comfortable to look at but it is not what other people see.

Questions people ask

Black previews, cameras in use elsewhere, and why your 1080p webcam sends 720p.

Why is my webcam preview black?

In order of likelihood: a privacy shutter or sliding cover still closed, another application holding the camera, the camera disabled in system privacy settings, or the wrong device selected in the list.

If the camera's indicator light is on but the picture is black, the shutter is the answer nine times out of ten.

Another app is using my camera. How do I release it?

Most operating systems allow only one application to hold a camera at a time. Fully quit Zoom, Teams, Skype, Discord and OBS rather than just closing their windows, because several keep running in the system tray.

On Windows, check Task Manager for background instances. A reboot clears any stubborn case in a few seconds.

Why is my resolution lower than the camera supports?

Browsers negotiate a stream rather than demanding the maximum. Bandwidth, lighting, USB port speed and the camera's own frame rate rules all push the negotiated resolution down.

A 1080p webcam on a USB 2.0 hub shared with other devices will often settle at 720p. Plugging directly into the computer usually restores it.

How do I switch between front and rear cameras?

Use the camera list, which fills in with real device names once permission is granted. On a phone, the front and rear cameras appear as separate entries.

Some phones expose several rear lenses, including ultrawide and telephoto, so you may see more entries than you expected.

Does this record or upload my video?

No. The video stream is displayed directly in the page and never leaves your device. Snapshots are drawn locally and are only saved if you choose to download one.

Pressing stop, or closing the tab, ends the stream and releases the camera.

Why is my frame rate low?

Light, almost always. In dim conditions a camera holds the shutter open longer for each frame, and a longer exposure means fewer frames per second. A 30 fps webcam routinely drops to 15 fps in a dark room.

A window or a lamp in front of you fixes it instantly and improves the picture more than a more expensive camera would.

Can I test a virtual camera from OBS or Snap?

Yes. Virtual cameras appear in the device list alongside physical ones, so you can confirm your scene is being output correctly before joining a meeting.

If a virtual camera is missing, its host application usually needs to be running and the virtual camera explicitly started.

The image is upside down or the wrong way round.

A mirrored preview is deliberate and normal. It feels natural to you, and video call apps do the same thing, while other participants see the unmirrored image. Toggle the mirror control to see what they see.

A genuinely upside-down picture is a driver or mounting issue, common on laptops where the module is installed inverted. The manufacturer's camera utility usually has a flip setting.

Which browsers support the webcam test?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera all support camera access on desktop, and Chrome and Safari support it on Android and iOS.

Camera access requires a secure connection. A page served over plain HTTP cannot open a camera at all, on any browser.

Does my camera work but look grainy and washed out?

That is the sensor struggling with light rather than a fault. Small webcam sensors raise their gain in dim rooms, and raised gain means visible noise and flat colour.

Light in front of you rather than behind you, and avoid sitting with a bright window at your back. Almost every bad webcam picture is a lighting problem.
